# RD2 weekly family digest

## Purpose
Youth baseball communication arrives in pieces: Playbook posts, RSVPs, schedule changes, uniform orders, payments. This skill turns a pile of messages into one short digest so nothing is missed and the family calendar is right.

## Inputs
- Pasted Playbook messages, screenshots, or schedule text. If screenshots, read them carefully and quote times exactly.
- Which kids and teams apply this week (for example, the RD2 11U Lentz roster and any 12U Cooperstown items). Ask if unclear.
- Optional: the family's known conflicts for the week (work events, church, other kids' games).

## Workflow
1. Extract every dated item: games, practices, evaluations, tournaments, arrival times, field addresses, uniform color, RSVPs due, payments due, volunteer asks.
2. Flag conflicts and changes: anything that moved since the last message, overlapping events across kids, early arrivals before work hours.
3. Pull out action items for Mike or Miriam with a due date: RSVP, pay, order, bring, sign.
4. Note anything unclear and draft a one-line question to send to the coach or Ryan Dent.
5. If Mike asks for tournament updates, summarize results and next game times only from what he provides or from an official tournament page he links. Do not guess scores or brackets.

## Output format (phone-friendly, drops into a text or email to Miriam)
- Headline: the single most important thing this week.
- This week: day, time, event, location, arrive-by, uniform.
- To do: owner, action, due date.
- Changes and conflicts.
- Questions for the coach (ready to paste).
- Offer calendar entries if a calendar connector is available. Create events only after Mike confirms.

Tone: warm, short, clear. No em dashes. Light bold. Never share kids' personal details outside the family digest.
