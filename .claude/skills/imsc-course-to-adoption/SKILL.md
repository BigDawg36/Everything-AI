# IMSC course-to-adoption follow-up

## Purpose
Courses only pay off when they turn into first cases. This skill converts one course event into a rep-owned follow-up plan anchored on Mike's coaching doctrine: one real surgeon, one real next step, one date.

## Inputs (ask only for what is missing)
1. Course name, date, location, sponsoring manufacturer, and products covered.
2. Attendee list: surgeon name, facility, IMSC territory, and owning rep. If Mike gives names only, map them to territories using the IMSC roster he provides. Do not guess ownership; mark "UNASSIGNED" and ask.
3. Notes per surgeon if available (Granola notes, rep notes): interest level, objections, current competitor, upcoming case types.
4. Optional: AcuityMD volume data for each surgeon (procedure volumes, facilities).

## Workflow
1. Sort surgeons into three tiers.
   - Tier A, ready: expressed intent or has a known upcoming case. Goal: first case within 30 days.
   - Tier B, interested: positive but no case identified. Goal: in-office or OR visit within 30 days.
   - Tier C, exposure only: attended, no signal. Goal: one value touch, re-evaluate at 60 days.
   If AcuityMD volume is provided, use it to rank within tiers.
2. Draft one follow-up email per surgeon for the owning rep to send from their own account. Under 120 words. Reference something specific from the course, offer one concrete next step (case support, tray availability, technique guide, OR visit), and propose two time options. No pricing, no clinical outcome claims, no comparisons.
3. Build the rep action tracker as a table: Surgeon | Facility | Territory | Rep | Tier | Next step | Due date | Status. Every row has one next step and one date. Due dates within 5 business days for Tier A, 10 for Tier B, 15 for Tier C.
4. Build a 30/60/90 scorecard: follow-ups sent, meetings held, first cases, repeat cases, by rep. Leave actuals blank for Mike to fill or pull later.
5. Draft a short note from Mike to the reps (under 150 words) that assigns the tracker, sets the check-in date, and states the expectation.

## Output format
- One section per rep, with that rep's surgeons, emails, and tracker rows, so Mike can forward each section as-is.
- Master tracker table at the end (drops cleanly into Excel or email). Offer an .xlsx if more than 10 surgeons.
- Tone: polished, professional, confident, respectful. Active voice. No em dashes. Light bold.

## Guardrails
- No PHI or patient case details in emails or trackers.
